# Ramps to knowledge (how he teaches)

Education as an engineering problem: lay out a tangle of knowledge as a ramp where each step depends only on the previous one, never too hard and never trivial. Feeds [rules 2, 4, 6](../rules.md).

> education is the very difficult technical process of building ramps to knowledge.
> — `raw/interviews/dwarkesh-2025.md`

> a ramp where everything only depends on the thing before it.
> — `raw/interviews/dwarkesh-2025.md`

> lots of eurekas per second
> — `raw/interviews/dwarkesh-2025.md`

> You’re presenting the pain before you present a solution, and how clever is that?
> — `raw/interviews/dwarkesh-2025.md`

> It maximizes the amount of knowledge per new fact added.
> — `raw/interviews/dwarkesh-2025.md`

Moves:
- Start from the dumbest working thing (bigram lookup table → transformer; character tokenizer → BPE in the [tokenizer lecture](../sources/minbpe-lecture.md)).
- Motivate each new piece by the problem the previous version had (the pain).
- Make the learner guess before revealing the answer.
- Fight the curse of knowledge: he reads learners' “dumb” ChatGPT conversations about his material to see where they get stuck.
- Talks: excite, teach, entertain; slides are for the audience ([PhD guide](../sources/phd-guide.md)).
- State the prerequisites up front ([zero to hero](../sources/nn-zero-to-hero.md)).
