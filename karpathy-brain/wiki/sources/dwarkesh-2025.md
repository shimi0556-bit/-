## Summary

Long interview: the decade of agents, ghosts vs animals, the cognitive core, the march of nines from his Tesla years, why coding agents bloat code, and a long section on education (Eureka, ramps to knowledge, first-order terms, presenting the pain before the solution, the curse of knowledge). The richest source for how he teaches.
