# Blue Ocean Discovery - Quick Reference

## When to Use

**Use this skill when:**
- Entering a market with established competitors
- Suspect large groups of people are NOT being served
- Industry offerings all look similar (red ocean)
- Want to challenge industry assumptions
- Preparing for solution-definition

**Don't use when:**
- You have no competitors yet (greenfield market)
- Looking for incremental improvements to existing product
- Already know exactly who your target is and why

---

## Three-Step Process

### Step 1: Strategy Canvas
Map what the industry competes on and where everyone looks the same.

### Step 2: Non-Customer Analysis
Identify who ISN'T buying and why (three tiers).

### Step 3: Four Actions Framework (ERRC)
Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create to define your Blue Ocean.

---

## Strategy Canvas (Step 1)

1. List 8-12 competitive factors
2. Rate 3-5 competitors on each (1-5 scale)
3. Find where curves converge (shared assumptions)
4. Find what no one invests in (blind spots)

---

## Non-Customer Tiers (Step 2)

| Tier | Who | Key Question |
|------|-----|-------------|
| **Tier 1** | Use minimally, ready to leave | What would make them leave? |
| **Tier 2** | Consciously refuse to buy | Why did they reject options? |
| **Tier 3** | Never considered the offering | What assumptions blind the industry to them? |

**For each tier:** Who are they? What do they use instead? What prevents buying? What would convert them?

---

## Four Actions / ERRC Grid (Step 3)

| Action | What It Means | Effect |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| **Eliminate** | Remove factors the industry takes for granted | Lower cost |
| **Reduce** | Reduce well below industry standard | Lower cost |
| **Raise** | Raise well above industry standard | Increase value |
| **Create** | Offer what the industry has never offered | Increase value |

**Value innovation = Eliminate/Reduce (cost down) + Raise/Create (value up) simultaneously**

---

## Inputs Needed

**Required:**
- Industry/product space to analyze
- Knowledge of 3-5 competitors

**Helpful to have:**
- Customer feedback data
- Market research
- Understanding of why people DON'T buy

---

## Outputs Produced

1. **Strategy Canvas** - competitive factors + value curves
2. **Non-Customer Map** - three tiers with barriers and conversion paths
3. **ERRC Grid** - specific factors to Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create
4. **New Value Curve** - how your offering differs from industry average
5. **Opportunity Summary** - actionable description of the Blue Ocean

---

## Time Estimate

**Full Blue Ocean Discovery:** 2-3 hours
- Strategy Canvas: 45-60 minutes
- Non-Customer Analysis: 45-60 minutes
- Four Actions Framework: 30-45 minutes

---

## Classic Example: Formula 1 Hotels (Accor)

**Eliminated:** Lobby, restaurant, reception staff, room decor
**Reduced:** Room size, furniture, amenities
**Raised:** Cleanliness, bed quality, quiet rooms, check-in speed
**Created:** Self-service kiosk check-in, modular rooms, budget-luxury feel

**Result:** 20% lower cost, 50% market share growth, served non-customers who couldn't afford traditional hotels

---

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Benchmarking, not innovating** - Goal is a DIFFERENT curve, not a better one
2. **Ignoring non-customers** - They're usually bigger than your current market
3. **Raising everything** - Must also Eliminate/Reduce to fund value creation
4. **Western bias** - Non-customers look different across global markets
5. **Creating without evidence** - New factors should come from non-customer insights

---

## Global Diversity Reminder

Non-customers are different across markets:
- US non-customer barrier might be price
- Nigeria non-customer barrier might be infrastructure
- India non-customer barrier might be cultural norms
- Japan non-customer barrier might be relevance

Consider non-customers in 2-3 different market contexts.
