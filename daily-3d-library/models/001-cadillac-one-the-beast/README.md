# #001 · Cadillac One “The Beast” (2018) · קדילק וואן ״החיה״

The US presidential state car, modelled procedurally in [`model.js`](model.js). Open [`index.html`](index.html).

**234 named parts in 12 systems, ~1,070 meshes, ~530K triangles, no model or image files.**

## Sources & accuracy

Public figures: weight 6.8–9.1 t, ~5.5 m long, ~1.8 m tall, 8″ doors, 5″ glass, GMC TopKick platform, 7 seats in 2-3-2, Goodyear run-flats. Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_presidential_state_car), [Tank Encyclopedia](https://tanks-encyclopedia.com/modern/usa/third-generation-custom-cadillac-presidential-state-car-the-beast/), [CarBuzz](https://carbuzz.com/the-beast-presidential-car/).

Most of the car's specifications are classified. The wheelbase (3.45 m), track, engine and transmission choice, interior layout and armour layering are informed reconstructions, not drawings. The seals and flags are stylised.
